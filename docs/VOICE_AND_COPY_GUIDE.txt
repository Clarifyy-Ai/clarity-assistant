CLARIFY AI — VOICE & COPY GUIDE
Source of truth for product tone and naming. Keep UI/help/support aligned.
Canonical names: src/lib/constants/productNames.ts (PRODUCT_NAMES).
================================================================================

TONE
  Calm, practice-first, non-jargon. Friendly under stress — short sentences,
  no blame, clear next step. Prefer "you can…" over "you must…".
  Avoid hype, sarcasm, and engineer-speak (tokens, latency, RPC, edge).

COMPLIANCE (always)
  Clarify AI is PRACTICE coaching — rehearse before the real interview.
  Never frame the product as a covert tool for live interviews.
  Prefer: "practice session", "rehearsal", "when you're practicing".
  Avoid: "during your interview", "they won't see", "undetectable".

BANNED ALIASES (do not use in UI, help, or support)
  Live Co-Pilot / Live Copilot → Practice Coach
  Live Rehearsal .............. → Practice Coach (or "practice session")
  Question Bank ............... → Answer Bank (gov "My Question Bank" is separate)
  Discrete UI ................. → Discrete mode (if the setting is needed)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LATENCY EXPECTATION (Overlay)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Hints typically appear in ~2–4 seconds after a clear question.
  Listening indicator tooltip already states this — keep it consistent.
  While waiting, show a calm status near hints: "Generating hints…" or
  "Generating full answer…" (or short "Generating…"). Never imply instant.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ERROR COPY PATTERNS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Mic permission
    Title: Microphone access needed
    Body: Allow the mic in your browser (or system) settings, then try again.
    CTA: Recheck audio / Open settings

  Offline
    Title: You're offline
    Body: We'll keep a simple template ready. Reconnect for full AI hints.
    Overlay badge tone: Offline template — real answer queued when back online.

  Credits exhausted
    Title: Out of credits
    Body: Top up or upgrade to keep generating hints and answers.
    CTA: View Credits & Usage / Upgrade
    Avoid: "payment failed", "quota exceeded", raw error codes.

  Session ended
    Title: Session ended
    Body: Nice work — your notes and scorecard are ready when you are.
    CTA: View session notes / Back to Practice Coach
    Never guilt the user for ending early.

GENERAL ERROR SHAPE
  1) What happened (plain words)
  2) What to do next (one action)
  3) Optional: where to get help (Help / support)
